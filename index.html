About Mangal Singh Brothers

Industry passion meets productivity.

Mangal Singh Brothers Private Limited (also known as “MSB”) is a market leader in high-quality power transmission belting.

Timing belts, Vee belts, flat belts and conveyor belts, alongside specialised textile machinery spares such as PU Discs for texturising machines and rubber fillet for looms — sourced from world market leaders in Japan and Europe.

For the manufacture of Cots & Aprons, we have entered into a technical collaboration with M/s. Kureha Elastomer of Japan. We supply Cots & Aprons to leading companies across industries in India.

Today we have emerged as a leading company in our sector. Our expertise lies in offering high-quality, price-competitive products to a wide spectrum of industries — our extensive product range enables us to service a wide range of customers, including original machine manufacturers as well as numerous prestigious end users.

Skill Set

Expertise in high-quality power transmission belting and precision textile machinery spares. Strong global partnerships with leading Japanese and European manufacturers, backed by deep technical knowledge and reliable customisation capabilities.

The Mission

To deliver world-class belting and textile machinery solutions that enhance performance, efficiency and reliability. Through global collaborations and a commitment to quality, we aim to be a trusted partner to industries across India.
